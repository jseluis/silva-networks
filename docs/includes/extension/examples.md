## Extend This Example

To turn this runnable example into a new SILVA construction:

1. identify the equilibrium state and every conditioning tensor;
2. isolate the repeated transition as its own module;
3. replace one source, operator, or readout at a time;
4. compare the new transition against an independent reference step;
5. retain a deterministic compact case with residual, error, and gradient assertions.

The complete authoring and scaling workflow is in
[Extending SILVA](../learn/extending-silva.md).
